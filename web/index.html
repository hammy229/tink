A storybook idea, made real

Tinker Bell had Peter.
Everyone deserves to be understood.

In Peter Pan, a sound like little bells could hold an entire thought—because someone knew how to listen.

For someone living with ALS, recovering from a stroke, or speaking a different language, a repeatable sound can carry a whole sentence. Tink helps the rest of the room understand.

A little magic, taught by you

Your own language
of sound.

No spelling boards. No training run. Teach Tink a handful of examples and it is ready to listen.

Chapter I

Teach a chime

Record a repeatable hum, click, sigh, or vowel about five times and add what it means.

Chapter II

Make the sound

Tink compares it with that person’s vocabulary and shows exactly how sure it is.

Chapter III
“I need help.”

Hear the meaning

Tink speaks the taught phrase—or translates it for the listener in the room.

The real product, not a mockup

Teach it. Try it.
Be understood.

The app below is live. Teach at least two sounds, then press Listen and make one of them. Your first run downloads the voc2vec model.
