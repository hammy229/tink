Spoken in her voice

Tink sits between a person and the room. It listens for the sounds they can still make, matches each one to a phrase they taught it, and says that phrase, so nobody has to guess.

For the person

Their words. Their voice.

Each phrase plays back as a recording of them saying it, banked while they still could.

For caregivers

Less guessing. Faster answers.

Urgent sounds raise an alarm until someone answers, and you can type a reply that Tink says aloud.

For privacy

Nothing leaves the laptop.

Audio, video, and the sound model stay on the device. No account, no cloud service.

How it works

Five recordings, then it listens.

  1. 01

    Teach a sound

    Record a hum, click, sigh, or vowel about five times and type what it means.

  2. 02

    Make the sound

    Tink compares it with that person’s sounds and shows how sure it is, including “I don’t know.”

  3. 03

    Hear the meaning

    Tink plays the phrase in their banked voice, or reads it aloud if none is banked yet.
